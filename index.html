Historical & Cultural Heritage

Opening Overview

The Grand Palace consists of a complex of royal halls, buildings, and palaces constructed in
exquisite traditional Thai architecture, showcasing remarkable craftsmanship and artistic detail.
Within its grounds is Wat Phra Si Rattana Satsadaram (the Temple of the Emerald Buddha), the royal
temple of the Grand Palace, which enshrines the Phra Phuttha Maha Mani Rattana Patimakorn (the
Emerald Buddha), the most revered Buddha image and a cherished national symbol of Thailand. The
buildings within the Grand Palace are arranged in an orderly manner, with the Inner Court clearly
designated as the private residential area for members of the royal family.

Grand Palace Bangkok / Wat Phra Kaew

The Golden Mount (Wat Saket)

It is one of the city's best-known viewpoints. The artificial hill was created during the reign of King
Rama III. A large chedi was originally planned but collapsed due to the soft ground. The hill was later
stabilized, and the current golden chedi was completed during the reign of King Rama V. From the top,
you'll get panoramic views of Bangkok, especially impressive around sunrise, sunset, or after rain
when the air is clearer.

Wat Arun Ratchawararam is regarded as a center of faith and an important symbol of both the
Thonburi and Rattanakosin eras. The temple houses an ordination hall (Ubosot) enshrining a Buddha
image in the Mara-Vijaya posture, along with various viharns and pagodas. Its entrance gates are
adorned with the famous white and green guardian giants of Wat Arun, which serve as protective
deities and reflect the temple’s rich beliefs and artistic beauty. In addition, Wat Arun is a popular
destination for tourists who come to admire its architectural splendor and learn about its historical
significance.

  • Dress Code at Temples

When visiting temples (Wat), please dress modestly. Shoulders and knees must be covered. Remove
shoes before entering temple buildings.

  • Transportation

Bangkok traffic can be heavy. We recommend using the BTS Skytrain, MRT Subway, or the Chao
Phraya Express Boat for the best experience.

  • Safety First

Keep an eye on your personal belongings in crowded areas. Always agree on the fare before
taking Tuk-Tuks or local taxis.
